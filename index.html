Sample league

A made-up, fully played season with full rosters, so you can see how every page looks with data in it.

Loads a full, finished 17-game season: all 272 games with box scores (every stat, leader and score), final standings, the 18-week schedule, season awards, transactions, an injury report and key dates. It also fills all 32 salary sheets with 53-man rosters and contracts, sets league caps, and adds free agents to Player Contracts. Player names are real; the contracts, games, numbers and moves are invented. A copy of everything you have now is kept, and Remove sample league puts it all back exactly.
