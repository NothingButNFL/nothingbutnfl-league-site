Paste draft pick rows Adds new picks and updates existing ones

Recognized columns, in any order: Year, Current (the team that owns the pick now; defaults to the original team), Round, Original, Notes. Teams can be abbreviations (KC), nicknames (Chiefs) or full names. A pick that already exists for that year, round and original team gets its owner and notes updated. Put an asterisk after the round for a compensatory pick (5*); those sort to the bottom of the round. Extra rows for the same year, round and original team are added as additional picks. Rows with a problem are skipped and listed.
