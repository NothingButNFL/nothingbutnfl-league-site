Upload every screenshot from one game's box score: the team stats pages and each player stats page (passing, rushing, receiving, defense, kicking). You can also drop or paste them here. Claude reads them, then you check the numbers before the game is added.

Reading box score screenshots works when this page is opened in Claude.

Paste Passing stats Updates matching teams and players
Claude reads the stat table in your screenshot and fills it in below for you to check. You can also paste a screenshot into the box or drop one here. Several screenshots of one long table can be uploaded together.

Any columns work: the page keeps your headers as they are. Include a Player column (or Team for team stats) and, if you can, a Team column so players link to their team. Each paste or screenshot updates the rows it contains: a team (or player) already in the table is replaced with the new numbers, new ones are added, and everyone else is kept. So you can add 11 teams from one screenshot, then the next 11 from another.
