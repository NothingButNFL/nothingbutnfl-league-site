    COMMISSIONERDraft setup

    1. Draft order

    From Draft Pick Manager (recommended): put the 32 teams in first-round order (whose pick comes first; Fill in reverse standings gives you a start), then Build from Draft Pick Manager. Every round's picks, who owns them after trades, and compensatory picks come from the manager, and any trade of these picks (a team's trade builder, Pending Trades, Draft Pick Manager, or Draft-day trade below) moves the pick here too.

    Or paste the order: your draft picks sheet straight in as Current, Round, Original, Pick Number (e.g. CHI, 1, CAR, 1: Chicago has Carolina's 1st-round pick). Pick Number can be the overall pick or the pick within the round. A * on the round means a compensatory pick in that round (BAL, 3*, BAL, 97). Or paste just the first-round order (32 teams) and the other rounds are built from Draft Pick Manager, or every pick as Pick, Team (from team).

    Skipped lines

      2. Draft pool

      One prospect per line: Rank, Name, Position, School (rank is optional). Adding keeps the players already in the pool.

      3. Combine results

      The 2026 combine results from Pro Football Reference are built in and show on Last Player Selected when the player is picked. To add or correct anyone, paste results here (from NFL.com's combine tracker or a spreadsheet); pasted results take the place of the built-in ones for that player. Put a header row first so the columns are matched, like Name, Ht, Wt, 40, Bench, Vert, Broad, 3-Cone, Shuttle (Arm and Hand work too). Without a header, that's the order used. Adding again updates players already in the list.

      4. Start over

      Removes every pick made in this draft. The order and the pool stay.

      SOUNDS

      Draft sounds

      Countdown Built-in beeps

      With 10 seconds left on the pick clock, a countdown plays: a beep each second, higher for the last three, and a horn at 0:00. To use your own countdown instead (an MP3, WAV or OGG file of about 10 seconds), choose it below; it starts at 10 seconds left. The file stays in this browser.

      When a pick is made Built-in chime

      A chime plays as each pick goes in, and the player flashes at the top of the page under Last Player Selected. Choose your own sound file to play instead.

      When a pick is traded Built-in trade sound

      Plays with the TRADE header on the draft ticker, only while the draft is live, on whichever page the trade is made (here, Draft Pick Manager, or approving it under Admin › Pending Trades).
