BY YEARS OF EXPERIENCE

Minimum player salaries

The league minimum for each experience bracket and season. When a team picks "Minimum" on a free agent offer, it becomes a 1-year deal at this amount for the current season, using the player's years of experience from Player Contracts.

Amounts in whole dollars, like 840,000 or 0.84M. Leave a box blank if that minimum hasn't been set. To paste from a spreadsheet, copy the cells, click the box where the top-left value goes and paste (Ctrl+V); if you copy the year headers and experience column too, each value lands in its matching box.

PRACTICE SQUAD

Practice squad weekly salaries

Weekly pay for practice squad players by years of experience and season. When a player joins a practice squad, his salary is this weekly amount × the regular-season weeks left, counting the current week.

Amounts in whole dollars per week, like 14,000. Leave a box blank if it hasn't been set; players then join at $0 until you set it and adjust their base salary.

VETERAN MINIMUM BENEFIT

VMB bonus limitation

The most a Veteran Minimum Benefit contract can include in signing bonus, roster bonus and other guaranteed compensation combined, by league year. VMB deals are for players with 4+ credited seasons, last one league year, pay the player's own minimum and count against the cap as the 2-year minimum.

Leave a season blank for no limit. 2026 starts at $187,500.
