CONTRACT MOVES

Restructure a contract

Lower this season's base salary (down to the player's league minimum) and roster bonus, and turn that money into signing bonus. It's spread evenly over the rest of the deal including this season, up to 5 seasons, and added to his existing signing bonus. Add void years to stretch it to 5. Requests go to the commissioner on Admin › Restructure Requests; nothing changes until it's approved.

04 / TRADE DESK

Trade center

Choose up to two partner teams, assign players or picks to each destination, review each team's cap impact, and propose the trade. Every partner team must accept, then the commissioner approves it on the Admin › Pending Trades page before players and picks move. Traded players' prorated bonuses stay with the sending team as dead cap: March 1–May 31, all remaining prorated bonuses hit this year's dead cap; June 1–February 28, this year's prorated bonus hits this year and future prorated bonuses go to next year's dead-cap table. The receiving team takes on the contract without prorated bonuses.

Build a trade

Select at least one partner team and add the assets each team will send.

Cap and pick records start blank. Enter your league's figures; no salaries, cap limits, or NFL transactions are preloaded as official data.
